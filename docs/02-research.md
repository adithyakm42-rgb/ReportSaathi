# ReportSaathi – Research Document

## 1. Project Overview

ReportSaathi is an AI-assisted healthcare application designed to help patients understand medical reports, laboratory results, prescriptions and discharge summaries in simple language.

The application aims to reduce confusion caused by complex medical terminology and make healthcare information easier to understand.

### Main Objectives

- Explain complex medical terminology in simple language.
- Help patients understand laboratory results using only the reference ranges printed on their reports.
- Clarify existing prescription instructions without changing medication dosages.
- Provide explanations in supported regional languages.
- Prepare useful questions that patients can ask their doctors.
- Identify potentially urgent information and display appropriate safety warnings.

### Target Users

- Patients who have difficulty understanding medical reports.
- Family members and caregivers who help patients.
- People who prefer healthcare explanations in their regional language.
- Users who need help preparing questions before visiting a doctor.

### Project Scope

ReportSaathi is intended to be an educational healthcare support tool. It will not diagnose diseases, prescribe medicines, modify medication dosages or replace professional medical advice.

## 2. Existing Medical Applications

This section examines existing healthcare applications to understand their features, advantages, limitations and possible opportunities for ReportSaathi.

### 2.1 MyChart

**Overview:**

MyChart is a patient portal developed by Epic Systems. It allows patients to access health information and communicate with healthcare providers through participating healthcare organisations.

**Main Features:**

- Access medical records and laboratory results.
- View medication lists and health information.
- Schedule and manage appointments.
- Communicate with healthcare providers.
- Access information through mobile and web applications.
- Support proxy access for authorised family members or caregivers.

**Advantages:**

- Provides convenient access to healthcare information.
- Helps patients manage appointments and medical records.
- Allows communication between patients and healthcare providers.
- Offers access to health information at different times.

**Limitations:**

- Medical reports may contain technical terminology that patients find difficult to understand.
- Laboratory results may display high or low indicators without providing sufficient explanation for every patient.
- Features and available records depend on participating healthcare organisations.
- Patients may need additional help understanding their medical information.

**Research Observation:**

MyChart provides strong medical record management and patient communication features. ReportSaathi could complement this type of service by focusing on explaining medical information in simple language.

## 4. Potential Common User Problems

Based on preliminary competitor research and the review of available healthcare applications, the following potential user problems have been identified.

### 4.1 Complex Medical Terminology

Patients may find it difficult to understand technical words, abbreviations and medical terminology used in laboratory reports and discharge summaries.

### 4.2 Confusion About Laboratory Results

Users may struggle to understand whether a laboratory value is within, above or below the reference range printed on their report.

### 4.3 Prescription Instructions

Some patients may find medical instructions difficult to understand, particularly when they contain unfamiliar terminology.

### 4.4 Fragmented Healthcare Information

Medical records and reports may be spread across different healthcare providers and applications.

### 4.5 Language Barriers

Patients may prefer explanations in their local or preferred language instead of technical English.

### 4.6 Difficulty Preparing Questions

Patients may not know what questions to ask their doctors after receiving medical reports.

### 4.7 Privacy and Security Concerns

Users may hesitate to upload sensitive medical documents without clear information about data storage, processing and privacy.

**Research Limitation:**

These are preliminary observations based on secondary research and competitor analysis. They should not be considered confirmed findings about all patients. Interviews, surveys and usability testing are required to understand actual user needs and priorities.

## 5. Privacy and Legal Considerations

ReportSaathi may process personal information contained in medical reports, prescriptions and discharge summaries. Therefore, privacy, security and responsible data processing must be considered from the beginning of development.

### 5.1 Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), 2023

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 establishes a framework for processing digital personal data in India. The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 provide additional details about implementing the Act.

Since ReportSaathi may process personal information through uploaded medical documents, the project should consider the following requirements:

- Obtain clear user consent before collecting and processing personal data.
- Collect only the information required to provide the application's services.
- Explain why personal information is being collected and how it will be used.
- Implement appropriate security measures to protect user information.
- Restrict access to personal information to authorised users and systems.
- Establish clear data retention and deletion procedures.
- Avoid sharing medical information with unauthorised third parties.
- Provide users with understandable privacy information.

**Implementation Timeline:**

The DPDP Act and Rules have a phased commencement schedule. Not all provisions become applicable at the same time. The main data-processing obligations are scheduled to commence 18 months after the November 2025 notification, in May 2027.

ReportSaathi should be designed with these requirements in mind rather than waiting until the relevant provisions become applicable.

**Source:**

https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa

**Important Considerations:**

- Use reasonable security practices.

ReportSaathi should consider implementing:

## 7. Go/No-Go Decision

**Decision: GO – Proceed with a Limited MVP**

Based on the preliminary competitor research, ReportSaathi has a potential opportunity to help patients understand medical documents more easily.

However, the project should initially focus on a limited set of features rather than attempting to provide complete healthcare assistance.

### 7.1 Recommended MVP Scope

- Upload medical reports and extract printed text.
- Explain medical terminology in simple language.
- Compare laboratory values only against reference ranges printed on the uploaded report.
- Prepare three to four questions for the patient's doctor.
- Provide explanations in selected supported languages.
- Display appropriate safety warnings when potentially urgent information is identified.

### 7.2 Major Risks

1. OCR may incorrectly extract medical values or units.
2. AI may generate inaccurate or misleading explanations.
3. Users may mistake the application for a diagnostic service.
4. Medical information may be exposed if privacy controls are inadequate.
5. The system may fail to identify potentially urgent information.

### 7.3 Conditions Before Release

- Test OCR accuracy using different medical report formats.
- Verify extracted values and units against the original document.
- Implement deterministic safety checks in the Python backend before calling the AI model.
- Prevent diagnosis, prescription generation, dosage modification and advice to stop medication.
- Never invent laboratory reference ranges.
- Obtain appropriate user consent and implement secure data handling.
- Conduct user testing and seek appropriate medical and legal review.

### 7.4 Final Decision

Proceed with the limited MVP, provided that accuracy, safety, privacy and usability requirements are validated before public release.

ReportSaathi must remain an educational support tool and must not replace qualified healthcare professionals.
